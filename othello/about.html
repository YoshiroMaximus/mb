About Othello

What is Othello?

Othello is a strategic board game for two players, designed to be simple to learn and complex to master. It is played on an 8x8 board with discs that are black on one side and white on the other.

History of the Game

Othello has its roots in the 19th-century game "Reversi," later refined and trademarked in the 1970s. The name comes from Shakespeare’s play, symbolizing duality and opposition.

Objective

The goal is to dominate the board by having the majority of your color discs at the end of the game. Flip your opponent’s discs by outflanking them strategically.

Rules

Basic Rules

  1. Players alternate turns, starting with black discs.
  2. A valid move flips at least one of the opponent’s discs.
  3. Pass your turn if no valid move is possible.
  4. The game ends when neither player can move.
  5. The player with the most discs wins.
